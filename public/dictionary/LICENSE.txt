Pronunciation: lemmas.txt names, for many words, a recording on Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/), and audio-credits.txt lists their authors and licenses.
The recordings themselves are not part of these files; the app loads each from Commons when
asked, under its own license, which the word's page in the app names with a link to its source.
