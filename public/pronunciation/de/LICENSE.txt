Vosli German pronunciation credits

Source: the recordings linked on German entries of German Wiktionary (https://de.wiktionary.org/), by its
contributors, data dump of 2026-11-01, via Kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org/dewiktionary/rawdata.html), each checked
against Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/) for its license and author.

Changes: reduced to the recordings the German dictionary uses, reformatted as sorted text.

The German dictionary (../../dictionary/de/lemmas.txt) names, for many words, a recording on Wikimedia Commons, and
credits.txt lists their authors and licenses. The recordings themselves are not part of these files; the app loads
each from Commons when asked, under its own license, which the word's page in the app names with a link to its source.
